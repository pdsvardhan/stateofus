ComWatch Direction Studio · v1

Four ways the same question can feel.

ComWatch isn't a survey — it's a loop: answer a question, instantly see what India thinks, learn something about yourself, then chase the next one. Below, that exact loop — Discover → Answer → Result + Insight — rendered in four visual languages. Same content, same structure, different soul. Pick one (or mix two) and I'll build the full clickable product in it.

Constant across all four
One question = one experience · mandatory personal insight on every result · 300–700ms reveal, never cinematic · anonymous, no accounts · India-centric framing · clean & abstract, no mascots.
What changes per direction
Colour temperature · type pairing · shape & corner language · how the result visualization is drawn · texture and weight · overall mood.
