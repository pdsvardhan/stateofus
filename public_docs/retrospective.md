# Retrospective — State of Us

## Keep
- The **experience-loop** framing (answer is rewarded, not just collected) — it is the whole differentiator.
- The **mandatory, template-driven personal-insight** layer — fast, trustworthy, and the reason results feel personal.
- **Anonymous-first** participation with skip always allowed — removes the friction that kills polls.

## Kill / cut
- Engagement mechanics (XP, streaks, infinite feed) — explicitly out; they optimize the platform, not the answerer.
- S/A/B tier *labels* in favor of descriptive labels; secondary visualizations that merely echo the primary.
- Generative insight text — replaced with templates after deciding the reward must never be able to lie.

## Learned
- Design deviations during the build were captured as an explicit decision record rather than smoothed over — the newspaper visual language needed real adjustment once interactions met live results.
- A uniform pipeline with per-question rendering is the right tradeoff: consistent to build, varied to read.

## Next
- Grow the launch bank to full coverage across all categories and interaction modes.
- Keep auth as migration *hooks* only until there's a reason to add accounts.
- Continue tightening the secondary-visualization rule so exploration never becomes an analytics rabbit hole.
