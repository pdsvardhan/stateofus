# Validation — Question Quality Framework

A public-opinion platform lives or dies on the quality of its questions. State of Us treats questions as **curated assets with a lifecycle** (draft → active → paused → frozen → archived), not user-generated content. Two artifacts govern that gate.

## Question Authoring Standard
A written standard every question must satisfy before going active: a clear single ask answerable in one or two lines, a neutral framing free of leading language, an interaction mode that fits the question's shape, and a result that will actually reward the answerer (no empty charts, no fake precision).

## Question Fit Evaluator
A checklist used to score a candidate question against the standard before promotion — does it fit one of the interaction modes, will the primary visualization read clearly, is geography relevant enough to justify a map, is the sample likely to be meaningful. Questions that fail are reworked or archived rather than shipped.

## What this proves
The bar is *editorial*, not algorithmic: questions are reviewed against an explicit standard before the public ever sees them. That is what keeps a launch bank of a hundred-plus questions from degrading into noise — and what separates "we collected answers" from "we asked good questions."
